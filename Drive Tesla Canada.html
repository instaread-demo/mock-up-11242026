Tesla ($TSLA) posts its biggest one-day gain since April 2025

Tesla shares closed up more than 11% on Monday after FSD v15 began rolling out, the stock's best day since April 2025.

Tesla shares [TSLA US$385.46 +11.46] jumped Monday after the company started sending Full Self-Driving v15 to a first group of owners over the weekend, and as technology stocks bounced back from last week’s slide.

Tesla finished the day at US$385.46, up US$39.64 or 11.46% from Friday’s close of US$345.82, according to Nasdaq.

How the day played out

Shares peaked at US$393.83, about 14% up, after two losing sessions.

Why the shares are moving

FSD v15 reached early access owners Saturday; Tesla cites “25x better handling” of safety-critical cases, with wide release due in December. Nvidia reinstating Jensen Huang as CEO also lifted tech stocks.

How JPMorgan is reading the update

JPMorgan’s Rajat Gupta sees Tesla leading consumer autonomy, per Yahoo Finance. Shares remain down about 14% this year; April 9, 2025 (+22.69%) still marks the last bigger one-day gain.
