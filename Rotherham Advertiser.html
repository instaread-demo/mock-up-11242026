“ I tell parents not to worry”: The Rotherham hairdresser helping neurodiverse kids into the barber’s chair

By Lyra AuldContributor
Published 23rd Nov 2026, 14:36 GMT

Laura Appleyard travels to families across the district to provide calm, home-based haircuts for youngsters who struggle in traditional salons.

For parents of kids with ADHD and Autism, haircuts can be “incredibly stressful”, so this Rotherham hairdresser has made it her mission to make them feel as “comfortable” as possible.

Laura first worked in a salon at 13, doing odd jobs. Now 36, she runs a mobile hairdressing business for children with additional needs across the district.

She has always enjoyed cutting children’s hair and now specialises in clients with ADHD and autism through Laura’s Mobile Barbering.

Anxious parents often contact her first, and she aims to reassure them.

She cuts hair in clients’ homes, explains each step beforehand and lets newcomers hear the clippers before going slowly.

Having swapped her own shop for mobile work, she finds helping children settle at home especially rewarding, recalling one family who was delighted after a home visit.
