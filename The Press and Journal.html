Fish firm John Ross Jr praise speedy reaction to ‘serious fire’ at Torry warehouse

Fire crews were called to the blaze at about 2pm on Saturday.

by Samuel Ukah &Jenna Scott
November 23 2026, 5:15 pm

Fish firm John Ross Jr has praised the quick reactions of the emergency services after its Torry warehouse caught fire on Saturday.

Four fire engines were sent to the blaze at about 2pm on November 21 after smoke started billowing from a storage warehouse.

The company produces Scottish smoked salmon at its home on Sinclair Road.

Crews were credited with limiting the damage, and the firm reopened on Monday.

CEO Christopher Leigh said nobody was hurt after the Saturday blaze and thanked the community, emergency services and staff.

The cause is still unconfirmed. The firm, smoking salmon by traditional methods since 1857, holds a Royal Warrant.
