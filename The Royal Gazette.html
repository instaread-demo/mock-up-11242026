Four new officers will be recruited in the war against financial offences including money laundering, it has been announced.

The consultant financial investigators added $550,000 to the budget of the Ministry of National Security, Dennis Lister III, the Junior Minister of Justice told the House of Assembly during a briefing on the supplementary budget on Friday.

The ministry’s total rise is $2.57 million for 2026-27: $650,000 current, $1.92 million capital.

Jarion Richardson, of the One Bermuda Alliance, asked about the investigators’ status and their NAMLAC (National Anti-Money Laundering Committee) title; Mr Lister cited their police support role.

Other queries went to the minister; Mr Richardson was dissatisfied with some answers on measurables.
