Witness T to testify partially in camera amid fears of violence

Madlanga Commission chairperson, retired Justice Mbuyiseli Madlanga, has ruled that Witness T will testify partially in camera on Tuesday with his face concealed and his voice distorted.

Witness T claims to have close personal knowledge of the operations of an alleged drug syndicate and its links to law enforcement. In his founding affidavit, Witness T describes a criminal network involved in drug trafficking, with alleged connections to police officers and prosecutors.

He is expected to detail the syndicate’s operations and officials’ alleged role, citing fears for his safety. He will appear remotely, with his identity and image withheld.
