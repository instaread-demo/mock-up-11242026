MO Governor Holds Press Conference on U.S. Army's Planned Detonation of Explosives in Iowa

"Based on the information we do have, we anticipate minimal impacts to Missouri. We believe there are no significant health risks to Missourians and livestock, " said Missouri Gov. Mike Kehoe (R) during a press conference in Jefferson City about how the state is getting ready for the Army's plan to destroy hazardous material by detonation at its ammunition plant in Iowa. Kehoe described the step as necessary for the safety of the base and nearby Iowa residents, while sharing neighboring governors' frustration over how the federal operation has been handled. Directors of several state agencies, among them Public Safety, Natural Resources and Agriculture, stood with him. Show Less
