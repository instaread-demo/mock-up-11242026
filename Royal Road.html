Writathon First Milestone & AI Update

11/16/2026, 6:28 PM
Listen to audio version of this article
0:00 / 0:00 Instaread
Sponsored by

The first writathon milestone is here, and it’s time to check out the list of stories that completed the first milestone by publishing at least 25,000 words since October 31st: https://www.royalroad.com/fictions/writathon/11

If you didn’t make the list, it doesn’t mean you are disqualified from the event, as you can still turn things around and write 55,555 words by Dec 5th. For more information about the rules, you can check this page: https://www.royalroad.com/author-dashboard/writathon

The biggest rule change this time is a ban on AI-generated entries, now enforced by an AI detector; this post explains the technical side.

A few changes:

  1. Every submission now passes through the detector, and authors are asked to label their own novels.

  2. Staff will not tag novels themselves, since enforcing tags with the detector would teach people to dodge it.

  3. Novels detected as human-written get a boost in the lists, such as Rising Stars.

  4. A new AI Translated tag sets AI-translated work apart from other AI-generated fiction.

Why do we care if it is AI or human written?

Royal Road first set out its rules in mid-2023, when it released its AI policy.

AI then posed little threat, and no reliable detector existed.

Some 3.5 years later, both AI writing and detection have improved sharply. Fear of wrongly labelling human work kept detectors off the site in 2023, but the new solution is 99.9962% accurate on human content, or 1 false positive in 26,000 checks.

Many human authors demanded action, while some AI users and readers asked why it matters.

Royal Road exists to connect authors and readers, and a flood of AI stories, comments and reviews risks a “dead internet” that crowds out human work.

The site has helped writers launch careers, yet human authors now struggle to be noticed amid the daily volume of AI novels.

So the team is backing human writing, calling it a pro-human rather than anti-AI stance.

Technical Details

Tested on 542,434 known human chapters, the detector flagged only 20 as AI, so it labels human work correctly 99.9962% of the time (roughly 1 in 26,000 misfires). It catches AI-generated content around 99.5% of the time.

It is not a generative model like Claude or GPT but a text classifier: pre-AI human chapters trigger different internal “neuron” patterns than AI-generated or AI-assisted ones.

It leans cautious, preferring to call AI text human-like over mislabelling it:

The categories can overlap, so the threshold favours marking content as human to limit false positives.

Good luck to all entrants, and congratulations to everyone who reached the first milestone.

EDIT: An FAQ was added for recurring questions.

FAQ

Q: Does adding an AI tag lower a story’s visibility on its own?
A: No. Modifiers follow the detector’s verdict, and properly tagged stories can still climb the lists.

Q: What if I am flagged by mistake?
A: Use writing software that records your full editing history online as proof.

Q: How do I hide AI-generated and AI-assisted stories?
A: Log in and use the global filters here: https://www.royalroad.com/home?globalFilters=true

Q: Does Grammarly count as AI-edited?
A: Basic spelling and clarity fixes do not trigger the detector, but Grammarly AI rewrites can, so turn off its generative features.
