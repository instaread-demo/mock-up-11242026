Focker-In-Law is, of course, the latest addition to the over-25-year-old Meet the Parents franchise, but it’s also a reunion for stars Skyler Gisondo, Beanie Feldstein, and Eduardo Franco after they shared the screen in Olivia Wilde‘s 2019 comedy Booksmart.

Speaking with Seen on the Screen host Jacqueline Coley, Gisondo looks back on that shared first film and calls reuniting eight years on “truly a really magical time.”

Since then, the trio’s credits have grown to include Stranger Things, Primetime and a Broadway revival of Funny Girl. Feldstein and Gisondo now take on Samantha and Henry Focker, roles Daisy Tahan and Colin Baiocchi held in 2010’s Little Fockers, with Franco as Samantha’s boyfriend, Eduardo Villalobos.

Franco says it was the first role his parents recognized instantly, and Gisondo, no stranger to superhero films, compares joining the franchise to landing one.

They also talk about Ben Stiller as their father Greg, whose choking fit gives the series’ classic dinner-table scene a new twist when Henry’s girlfriend Olivia Jones (Ariana Grande) steps in to help.

Feldstein praises Stiller’s commitment in that scene and says director John Hamburg kept her improvised line calling Greg by his real name, Gaylord.

The video also quizzes the three on Meet the Fockers trivia and names the castmate who made everyone laugh most.
