From 90s Nostalgia to Ghostly Fun: 5 Reasons to Watch Sony AATH’s New Shows

There’s something special about stories that bring back old memories, make us laugh and offer a little escape from the everyday. Sony AATH is all set to bring two new fiction shows – Dear 90’s and Bhoot Ginni Jilipilata From 1942. While one takes viewers on a nostalgic trip to 1990s Bengal, the other promises a fun mix of fantasy, humour and family drama. Premiering on 23 November 2026, both shows have plenty to offer. From Koel Mallick’s television fiction debut to an unusually bossy little ghost, here are five reasons to look forward to these new shows.

1. Two Shows, Two Very Different Worlds

Dear 90’s revisits letters, cassettes and first romance; Bhoot Ginni Jilipilata From 1942 offers ghostly comedy.

2. Koel Mallick Is All Set for Her Television Fiction Debut

Bengali film star Koel Mallick makes her first move into TV fiction as Aparajita, alongside Angshuman Maitra and Anindita Jana.

3. Meet Jilipi, the Seven-Year-Old Ghost Who Thinks She Runs the House

Sreejoyee Majumdar plays Jilipi, a strong-willed child ghost who tries to boss around a family whose world has changed since her time.

4. Entertainment for the Entire Family

Each show centres on family, through growing up or through supernatural humour.

5. Weekday Entertainment Is Sorted

From 23 November 2026, both air Monday to Friday on Sony AATH, with the 6:30 PM slot for Bhoot Ginni Jilipilata From 1942 and 7:00 PM for Dear 90’s.

Both are on Sony AATH and Sony LIV.
